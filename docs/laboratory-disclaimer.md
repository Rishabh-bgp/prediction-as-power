# Laboratory disclaimer

The manuscript reports magnitudes from a synthetic, structurally calibrated hourly laboratory of a Germany-like zone.

## May be discussed as laboratory results

- Day-ahead AI wind MAE of about 5,971 MW versus 9,078 MW for the NWP-style regime
- Mean absolute counterfactual price shift of 12.90 EUR/MWh
- Stylised imbalance-cost reduction of 3.33 million EUR per year on a 100 MW slice
- 254 avoided false committed hours on a 500 MW combined-cycle unit

## Must not be cited as official statistics

Those figures are not ENTSO-E, Bundesnetzagentur, or ACER statistics. They are produced by a generator that does not use a Transparency Platform token.

## Institutional claims that do not depend on the generator

- SDAC clears expected residual load.
- SIDC trades forecast revisions.
- Balancing prices the remainder.
- The slope of price in expected wind is non-positive and steeper in tight hours.
- Public transparency socialises a baseline; private models capture a residual.
